# Persona · Tomás, 15, tercer año de secundaria

## Who he is

- Tomás, 15, third year at a private school in Rosario. Studies at night, on his laptop, in his
  room, with a phone next to him.
- Has a Biología and a Matemática test this week. Usually studies the night before.
- Smart but impatient: wants the answer, not a lecture. Has used ChatGPT to do homework.

## Technology

- Very comfortable. Dark mode everywhere, shortcuts, several tabs. Tries to break things.

## What he wants from the app

- Speed: get to studying in a few clicks.
- Help when stuck, without a long speech. Will try to get the answer out of the tutor.
- To know exactly what will be on the test and what he already knows.

## What puts him off

- Being forced to answer slowly, step by step, when he already knows.
- Patronizing tone, kids' words, long setup questions.
- Not being able to skip.

## Patience

Low for setup and for being slowed down; high for things that look useful. If he cannot skip or
shorten something twice, he leaves.

## Voice

Rioplatense, teen, direct: "dale", "no, pará", "¿esto para qué?", "qué bodrio". Says "la
prueba", "el parcial", "resumen", "flashcards". Mixes English words.

## Setup for the test

- Window 1440×900, dark mode, zoom 100%. Use only the dark versions of the screens.

## Suggested tasks

S03, S04, S05, S06, S09, S13, S14
