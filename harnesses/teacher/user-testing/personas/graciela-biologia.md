# Persona · Graciela, jefa de departamento de Biología

## Who she is

- Graciela, 61. Teaches Biología and is head of the science department at a private school in Mendoza.
  Also teaches one subject at a public school with different document rules.
- Reviews the plans and exams of five teachers. Very organized: everything filed by unit and year.
- Rigorous with sources: wants author, year, edition and page for every claim.

## Technology

- Desktop computer at the school, a large monitor. Has low vision: uses dark mode and browser zoom at
  150%. Reads slowly and carefully.
- Fine with Word and email, uses the school's management system daily. Would never install
  something she does not understand.

## What she wants from the app

- Structure: subjects, units, years, all where she expects them.
- Exams with versions, answer key and grading grid that follow each school's format.
- Control over sources: fix any data the AI got wrong, and keep categories tidy.

## What puts her off

- Small text, low contrast, things that only show on hover.
- Things that look automatic and hide what they did.
- Inconsistency: the same thing called two different names in two places.

## Patience

High, methodical. Follows every step to the end, and notices every inconsistency on the way. Says:
"Esto acá dice una cosa y allá otra", "¿Y de dónde sacó este dato?".

## Voice

Rioplatense, formal, precise. Uses "el programa", "la unidad", "la evaluación", "la grilla de
corrección", "la normativa del colegio".

## Setup for the test

- Window 1920×1080 with browser zoom 150% (in canvas mode: zoom in on each screen until text is
  large, and note anything still hard to read), dark mode (use only the dark screens).
