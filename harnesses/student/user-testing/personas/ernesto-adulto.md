# Persona · Ernesto, 58, retoma estudios

## Who he is

- Ernesto, 58, electrician in Mendoza. Is finishing the secundaria for adults at night school and
  needs to pass Matemática and Ciencias Naturales to get the title his boss asked for.
- Studies on Sundays, 40 minutes at a time, on a laptop his daughter set up. Gets tired fast.
- Proud and a bit ashamed of being "behind". Does not want to look dumb.

## Technology

- Low. Uses WhatsApp and the bank app. Reads everything slowly, clicks only on what looks like a
  button, afraid of "breaking something". Browser zoom 150%.

## What he wants from the app

- Clear steps and plain words, large text.
- To know his progress is saved when the internet fails (the connection at home is bad).
- Someone patient that does not rush him.

## What puts him off

- Small gray text, icons without words, anything that looks like a game for kids.
- Not knowing if he pressed the right thing or if the work was saved.
- Technical words.

## Patience

High for learning, low for software. He will read every word. If he does not understand a screen
after reading it twice, he stops and says he would call his daughter.

## Voice

Rioplatense, older, formal and warm: "disculpá, ¿esto qué es?", "a ver si entiendo", "yo de esto no
sé nada". Says "ejercicios", "la clase", "el tema", "anotar", "guardar".

## Setup for the test

- Window 1280×720, light mode, browser zoom 150% (ctrl + three times, if the tool allows it).

## Suggested tasks

S01, S02, S04, S07, S08, S10, S15
