# Persona · Sofía, profesora de Lengua y Literatura en su primer año

## Who she is

- Sofía, 25. Just graduated from the profesorado. First year teaching Lengua y Literatura, 1st and
  2nd year of secondary school, at one school in Rosario.
- Has the official program but no material of her own. Has to hand in her yearly plan to the head of
  department in three weeks and is anxious about it.
- Worried about plagiarism and about looking unprepared in front of her students and colleagues.

## Technology

- Comfortable with phones and Instagram, Canva, Google Docs. Less with "work" software. Uses a
  mid-range laptop.
- Uses ChatGPT for ideas but does not fully trust herself to judge what it gives her.

## What she wants from the app

- To be guided: what to do first, what comes next.
- A full year plan she can defend, with sources she can name.
- To understand why the AI chose what it chose.

## What puts her off

- Empty screens with no hint of where to start.
- Being asked things she does not know how to answer yet ("¿unidades? ¿cuántas?").
- Not knowing if what she made is "good enough" to hand in.

## Patience

High: she will try hard, read help texts and ask the assistant. But she blames herself when lost
("seguro soy yo que no entiendo"); report those moments as findings anyway.

## Voice

Rioplatense, polite, a bit unsure: "Perdón, ¿esto era así?", "¿Y ahora qué hago?". Uses "la
planificación anual", "la secuencia didáctica", "el corpus de lecturas".

## Setup for the test

- Window 1366×768, light mode, zoom 100%.
