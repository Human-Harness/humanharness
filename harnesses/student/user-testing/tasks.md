# Task bank (student)

Each task is a goal the way a learner would say it, with no UI words, so the test shows whether the
learner finds the way alone. Do only the tasks you were given. Adapt the details (topic, subject) to
your persona when the mockup lets you; the goal stays the same. A task is done when the goal is met
as the persona understands it, not when a given screen is reached.

## Starting out

- **S01 · First day.** You just installed the app. Get it ready for you and reach the point where
  you could start studying.
- **S02 · Your first topic.** You have a topic to learn and photos of your notebook. Get the app to
  know about it.
- **S03 · Fix the plan.** The app made you a list of what to learn, in an order. One item is not
  something you need, and you think one is missing. Change it.

## Studying

- **S04 · A study session.** You have about half an hour. Start studying the topic.
- **S05 · Stuck.** In the middle of studying you do not understand a question. Ask for help, and
  find out what happens if you keep not understanding.
- **S06 · Not working for me.** The way the app is teaching you is not working. Try another way
  without losing what you did.
- **S07 · Finishing.** Time is almost up or you want to stop. End the session and find out what you
  got out of it.
- **S08 · Running out of time.** The time you chose is over and you want to keep going.

## Remembering

- **S09 · Daily review.** The app says there is something to review today. Do it.
- **S10 · What did I study?** You want to see what you did last week and look something up that you
  remember writing.
- **S11 · Am I learning?** Find out how you are doing on a topic and what "learned" means here.

## Changing and settings

- **S12 · New page.** You took a new photo of your notebook. Add it and see whether your plan
  changes.
- **S13 · A test is coming.** You have a test in a few days. Get ready for it.
- **S14 · Change how it talks to me.** The hints are too short (or too long) and the tone does not
  fit you. Change it.

## When things go wrong

- **S15 · No internet.** The connection drops while you are studying. Know what happened to your
  last message and keep going.
- **S16 · Usage limit.** The app says you cannot use it more for now. Know what is saved and when
  you can come back.
