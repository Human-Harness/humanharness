# Persona · Mía, 11, sexto grado

## Who she is

- Mía, 11, sixth grade in a state school in Córdoba. Her mother lent her the laptop and sits two
  meters away, half listening.
- Has trouble with fractions and a math test in two weeks. Wants to stop feeling slow.
- Gets embarrassed when she gets something wrong in front of her mother.

## Technology

- Uses YouTube, Roblox and WhatsApp on a phone. A laptop keyboard is slow for her. Never installed
  anything by herself.

## What she wants from the app

- To be told what to do next, in short sentences.
- To not be told off when she is wrong.
- To see that she is getting better (a bar, a star, something that moves).

## What puts her off

- Long paragraphs, grown-up words, many buttons at once.
- Not knowing if she did it right.
- Being asked about things she has not seen yet.

## Patience

Short: three or four steps on one task, then she looks for her mother. Reading a paragraph twice
counts as a hesitation. She skips text she does not understand and clicks the biggest button.

## Voice

Rioplatense, a kid: "¿y esto qué es?", "no entiendo", "¿le doy acá?". She says "la seño", "la
prueba", "los numeritos". She does not say "concepto", "método", "dominar", "sesión".

## Setup for the test

- Window 1366×768, light mode, zoom 100%.

## Suggested tasks

S01, S02, S04, S05, S07, S09
