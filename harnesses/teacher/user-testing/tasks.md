# Task bank

Each task is a goal the way a teacher would say it, with no UI words, so the test shows whether the
teacher finds the way alone. Do only the tasks you were given. Adapt the details (subject, year,
topic) to your persona; the goal stays the same.

A task is done when the goal is met as the persona understands it, not when a given screen is
reached.

## Starting out

- **T01 · First day.** You just installed the app and have never used it. You have a class
  tomorrow. Get to a first class you could actually give.
- **T02 · Find something fast.** Last week you prepared something about a topic you remember (for
  example "Revolución de Mayo" or "fracciones"). Find it again without browsing.

## Subjects and units

- **T03 · Set up a subject.** Add one of the subjects you teach this year, at the school where you
  teach it.
- **T04 · Organize by units.** Your program has units. Add the first unit to a subject.
- **T05 · Reuse last year.** You gave this subject last year. Bring last year's classes into this
  year without redoing them.
- **T06 · New school year.** The year is over. Get next year ready, choosing what to keep from this
  one.
- **T07 · Old subjects.** You stopped teaching a subject two years ago and want to look at what you
  had for it.

## Classes

- **T08 · A class from scratch.** Prepare a class on a topic of your subject, using sources you
  trust.
- **T09 · Ask for a class.** Get the app to prepare a class for you just by telling it what you
  need, the way you would ask a colleague.
- **T10 · Fix a slide yourself.** One slide of a class has a wrong date. Fix it by hand.
- **T11 · Change a class with help.** The class is too long for your 40-minute slot. Get it
  shortened.
- **T12 · Ready to give.** You checked a class and it is fine. Mark it as ready.
- **T13 · Take it to class.** Get the class out of the app in a form you can project or print,
  with the bibliography included.
- **T14 · The plan for the students.** Find what the class gives the students (the class plan,
  the students' handout) and check it.

## Sources

- **T15 · Add a source.** Add a book chapter you use a lot, so the app can cite it.
- **T16 · Add a photocopy.** You only have a scanned photocopy of a text. Add it.
- **T17 · Find more sources.** You need more material for a topic. Get the app to look for some,
  and keep only the good ones.
- **T18 · Check a source.** Open a source you added and get the original file back.
- **T19 · Wrong author.** A source shows the wrong author. Fix it.
- **T20 · Tidy your sources.** Your sources are getting messy. Group them in a way that makes sense
  to you, and merge two groups that are really the same.

## Exams

- **T21 · Exam from classes.** Make an exam covering the last three classes.
- **T22 · Exam from material.** Make an exam using only two specific texts, not the classes.
- **T23 · Fix a question.** One exam question has two right answers. Fix it.
- **T24 · Print the exam.** Get the exam ready to hand out: two versions, the answer key and a
  grading grid.
- **T25 · All my exams.** See every exam you have, across all your subjects.

## Planning

- **T26 · Plan the year.** Get a plan for the whole year of a subject, and turn it into classes.
- **T27 · What's pending.** It is Sunday night. Find out what you still have to check before
  Monday.

## Settings

- **T28 · A new school.** You started teaching at another school. Add it, with its logo and the way
  it wants documents to look.
- **T29 · Citation style.** Your school asked you to cite in APA. Make the app do that.
- **T30 · One subject, another style.** One subject must use a different document style than the
  rest of that school. Set it.
- **T31 · Add an extra.** Someone told you the app can be extended with an add-on. Install one.

## The assistant

- **T32 · Ask how.** You do not know how to do something (for example, change your school's logo).
  Ask the app.
- **T33 · More room.** The help panel takes too much space on your small laptop. Get the space back,
  and then bring the help back.
- **T34 · Something fails.** While the app is preparing something, the internet drops (in canvas
  mode: look for what the app would show). Find out what was kept and how to continue.
