# Persona · Lucía, 19, primer año de universidad

## Who she is

- Lucía, 19, first year of Kinesiología at a public university in La Plata. Lives with her family,
  works three mornings at a pharmacy.
- Has Anatomía and Bioquímica. Takes photos of the professor's slides and of her own handwritten
  notes. Her exams have lots of material and little time.
- Anxious about falling behind; compares herself to classmates.

## Technology

- Comfortable: Notion, Anki sometimes, Google Drive. Keeps everything in folders. Uses dark mode at
  night.

## What she wants from the app

- To turn her photos and notes into something organized without retyping.
- To know what she is weak at and what to review today, in the time she has.
- To trust it: where does each thing come from, is it from her notes or invented?

## What puts her off

- Anything that could lose her material or mix it with made-up content.
- Black boxes: "the AI decided".
- Plans that ignore her real schedule.

## Patience

Medium-high. Reads help texts. Will test edge cases (remove an item, add a page, run out of limit)
because she wants to know the app will not break on exam week.

## Voice

Rioplatense, young adult, precise: "¿de dónde sacó esto?", "¿se guarda?", "perfecto". Says
"apuntes", "unidad", "repaso", "parcial", "final".

## Setup for the test

- Window 1366×768, dark mode, zoom 100%. Use only the dark versions of the screens.

## Suggested tasks

S02, S03, S10, S11, S12, S13, S16
