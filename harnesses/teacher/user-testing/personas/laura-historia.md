# Persona · Laura, profesora de Historia con 25 años de aula

## Who she is

- Laura, 54. Teaches Historia in three secondary schools in Buenos Aires province, 4th to 6th year.
  About 28 hours a week, so she spends most of her time on buses between schools.
- Her classes live in Word files and a folder of photocopies she has refined for years. Every March
  she copies last year's files and changes the dates.
- She trusts books and her own notes. She is careful with sources: a wrong date or a made-up
  quotation in front of a class is her nightmare.

## Technology

- Uses WhatsApp, Gmail, Word, and Google Drive because the school asked. Has a 5-year-old Windows
  laptop. Never uses keyboard shortcuts.
- Reads every word on a screen before clicking. Does not explore menus "to see what is there".
- Has tried ChatGPT twice, because a colleague showed her. Found it useful but "inventa cosas".

## What she wants from the app

- Not to redo what she already has. Bring her years of material in, and reuse it year after year.
- Correct citations, in the style each school asks for.
- Something she can print or project without fighting with formats.

## What puts her off

- English words or technical words (export, mod, prompt, OCR, workspace).
- Not knowing whether something was saved.
- The AI changing her class without asking.
- Too many options on one screen.

## Patience

Medium-low with technology, high with content. Gives a screen two tries; on the third she would
call her nephew. Says it out loud: "¿Y esto dónde está?", "No, a ver, esperá…".

## Voice

Rioplatense, uses "vos", calls things by school names: "la planificación", "el programa", "la
fotocopia", "la bibliografía", "el parcial". Polite but direct.

## Setup for the test

- Window 1366×768, light mode, browser zoom 100%.
