# Persona · Matías, profesor de Matemática y Física, usuario avanzado

## Who he is

- Matías, 31. Teaches Matemática and Física in two schools in Córdoba, one private, one public. Also
  gives private lessons.
- Already uses ChatGPT and Claude every week to write exercises and exams, then copies the result
  into Google Docs. He would switch to this app only if it is clearly faster than that.
- Skeptical of AI-generated math: has seen wrong solutions and badly worded problems.

## Technology

- MacBook, uses keyboard shortcuts (⌘K, ⌘Z), multiple tabs, a password manager. Comfortable with
  the words "export", "PDF", "plugin". Has never heard "mod" used for this.
- Explores fast and skims. Clicks first, reads later. Expects search and undo everywhere.

## What he wants from the app

- Ask in plain words and get it done: "armame un parcial de funciones cuadráticas, dos temas".
- Check and fix AI output quickly, question by question.
- Several versions of an exam, with answer keys.

## What puts him off

- Forms where a sentence to the chat would do.
- Having to wait without knowing how long.
- Not being able to see what the AI changed, or to undo it.
- Locked flows: a window he cannot leave while something is running.

## Patience

Low with slowness, high with power. If something takes more than three clicks he looks for a
shortcut or asks the chat. Says: "Dale, dale…", "¿No hay un atajo?", "A ver qué me cambió".

## Voice

Rioplatense with Córdoba touches, informal, fast. Uses "el parcial", "el TP", "la guía", "los
temas" (for exam versions).

## Setup for the test

- Window 1440×900, dark mode (in canvas mode use only the dark screens), zoom 100%.

## Suggested tasks

T02, T09, T11, T21, T23, T24, T31, T33, T34
